# Nudgely privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Nudgely is a student project by Team Bongo Gang for the AI-GNITION 2026 competition. It helps a small shop send personal WhatsApp follow-ups to customers who have chosen to message it, and measures whether those messages bring customers back.

## What we collect
When you message a shop that uses Nudgely on WhatsApp, we receive:
- your WhatsApp phone number and profile name
- the messages you send to the shop, and the time you sent them
- whether our messages to you were delivered or read

For the demo shop "Kampung Fresh", customer names and chat histories shown in the app are fictional. Real messages come only from people who chose to message our test number, such as our own team and demo participants.

## How we use it
- to reply to you and to send you follow-up messages from the shop
- to tag what you asked about (for example a restock request) so the shop's reply is relevant
- to measure, in aggregate, whether follow-up messages lead customers to return

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services needed to run Nudgely:
- **Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API)**, to send and receive messages
- **an AI model provider**, to tag messages and draft replies. Names and phone numbers are removed from the text before it is sent

## Your choices
- **Stop messages:** reply **STOP** at any time and we will stop messaging you.
- **Delete your data:** contact us through the link below and we will delete your messages and number within 7 days.

## How long we keep it
Messages are kept only for the competition demo and pilot, and deleted by **31 December 2026**.
