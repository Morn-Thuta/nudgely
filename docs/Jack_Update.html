Hackathon 21–23 Oct

  • All 30 messages generated, then hand-checked twice: fixed 5 that claimed things the chats don't support, and rewrote 26 for a respectful tone ending in a question.
  • Evidence verifier: a message is flagged if it says something the chat doesn't back up. No draft is ever written for an opted-out customer.
  • Fixed the Gemini client and the Windows crash in the LLM scripts.

The AI engine 3–5 Nov

  • Reply bot: answers a customer or hands to a person. A checker blocks invented prices or offers, and a blocked reply can never be sent by mistake. HUMAN always reaches a person.
  • Per-message tagging, fast enough for a live webhook, measured on a 32-chat test set.
  • Template filling: the 6 WhatsApp templates are filled by code. A blank the model invents is rejected, not patched.
  • Model fallback: when one free model is rate-limited it moves to the next, without hanging.
  • A model a real shop can use: retrained on 10 features computable from phone, date and amount. It lost no accuracy doing it (57% vs 44.8% for the 190-column one; the gap is within the noise).
  • Shop scorer from a sales export, and a daily job that runs the whole pipeline unattended.
  • Campaign outcome logging with the kept-back group, and docs/JACK_WORK.md explaining each piece.

The app 6–8 Nov

  • Landing page with sign-in and an "Explore the demo" door, plus the rotating 3D customer field.
  • Sidebar and page layout: one job per page, sign out at the top, a clear message when a campaign runs.
  • Campaign plan rewritten so a shop owner understands it, with the message list collapsible.
